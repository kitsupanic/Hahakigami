# Code of conduct

Hahakigami should be a project anyone can take part in without being harassed or talked down to. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and that the maintainer makes the final call on what is merged.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or harassment of any kind
- Discriminatory remarks about gender, sexual orientation, disability, race, religion, age, nationality or appearance
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing a discussion

## Reporting

Use the **Report content** option in the `…` menu on the issue, pull request or comment. This reaches GitHub and the maintainer without posting anything in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared.
