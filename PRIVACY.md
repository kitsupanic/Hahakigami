# Privacy

Hahakigami and Hahakigami Lite collect no data.

- No accounts, analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
- Scans read file metadata (names, sizes, dates) on your Mac. File contents are never read, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- Scan results, the Cleanup queue and Allowed locations are stored only on your Mac, in the app's own data folder.
- The apps make no network requests.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/kitsupanic/Hahakigami/issues.
